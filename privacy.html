Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

The short version

  • Your photos and videos never leave your iPhone or iPad. Memories are made on the device.
  • We keep what the app needs to plan your holidays: your family's preferences, your holidays and your plans.
  • We don't show adverts, track you across apps or websites, or sell your information.
  • AI suggestions see your family's preferences, children's age ranges and where you're staying, never names or photos.
  • Deleting your account in the app deletes everything we hold about you. You can also download a copy of it in the app.

Who we are

Vayanza is provided by Antonio Pagliara, a sole trader trading as Vayanza (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal information described here.

Contact: hello@vayanza.app

What we collect and why

Your account

You sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an identifier for your account, which we link to a random Vayanza account number. We don't ask Apple for your name or email address and we don't keep them. We store a sign-in token from Apple, encrypted, so we can end the link with Apple when you delete your account.

Your family

What you tell us in family setup: a family name, the names or nicknames you give each person, whether each is an adult or a child, and an age range (never a date of birth). Also your interests and dislikes, budget, pace, how far you're happy to travel, preferred start time and downtime, and whether you use public transport. We use these to choose and explain suggestions. Preferences the app learns from your feedback are shown to you, and are kept only when you confirm them.

Dietary and mobility needs

If you choose to add dietary or mobility needs, they may say something about someone's health. They're optional. We use them only to filter and explain suggestions, with your explicit consent, which you give by adding them. You can remove them at any time in family setup.

Your holidays

Destinations, dates, time zones, where you're staying (a hotel's name or an address you type), ideas you save or reject, the days you plan, reservations you add, routes you ask for, and your ratings and feedback.

When you search, we keep the search (the topic you chose, or what a typed question was about, never its words) and its results for up to 30 days, so you can come back to them. A question you type is sent to find answers and not stored.

Your location

Only when you ask for something near you, such as “near me” suggestions or Today. The app asks for your location while you're using it, never in the background. Your position is sent with that request to find nearby places and kept, rounded to about 11 metres, with that search for up to 30 days; it isn't saved to your holidays. For Today's weather we use only a rough area, rounded to about 11 kilometres.

Photos and videos

If you allow Photos access, the app reads your photos' dates and places on your iPhone or iPad to match them to your holiday. Your photos, videos and highlight videos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and are never used to train AI. What the app remembers about them (which photo belongs to which day) stays on your device.

Purchases

Apple handles payment; we never see your card details. Apple sends us the purchase's transaction details (product, dates, renewals, refunds) so we can unlock your plan or Trip Pass, and an anonymous account token so a purchase is matched to the right account.

Device check

To stop scripts misusing paid features, the app uses Apple's App Attest to show our server that requests come from the genuine Vayanza app on a real iPhone or iPad. We store the key App Attest creates for the app on your device, and no device identifier.

Feedback you send

If you send feedback about the app, we keep your answer, any comment you write and the app and iOS versions for 12 months.

Anonymous usage counts

We keep anonymous daily totals of how the app is used, such as how many searches were made, and anonymous sales totals. They don't identify you and stay after an account is deleted.

Technical records

Our servers record requests (time, account number, what kind of request, whether it worked) to keep the service secure and fix problems. They don't record what you searched for or your family's details. If you share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple sends us crash reports (device model, iOS and app versions and where the app stopped).

Outside the UK

OpenAI and Apple may process information in the United States. Where they do, it's protected by safeguards UK law approves, such as the UK–US data bridge or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, family and holidays: until you delete them or your account.
  • Searches and their results, and suggested day arrangements: up to 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Feedback you send about the app: 12 months.
  • Sign-in sessions: 30 days.
  • Technical records: 14 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as the law requires us to keep business records (usually six years), without your family's details.
  • Backups: our database backups roll over within 30 days. If we ever restore one, we delete again anything deleted since it was made.

When you delete your account in the app (Account › Delete account), we delete your account, family, holidays, plans, feedback and any unexpired Trip Pass from our servers straight away, and end the link with your Apple sign-in. The app also clears what it holds on your device. We keep only the deleted account's random number, for 31 days, so a restored backup can't bring it back, and backups roll over within 30 days. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it in Settings first. You can also delete a single archived holiday permanently in the app.

Children

Accounts are for adults. A parent or guardian can add their children to the family, by name or nickname and an age range only, so suggestions suit them. Children don't have their own accounts and we never ask for a child's date of birth.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable form. Most of it you can see, change and delete in the app yourself, and you can download a copy of what we hold in Account settings › What's stored where › Download your data. Email hello@vayanza.app for anything else; we'll reply within a month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Security

Your information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted, and our servers check on every request that you can only see your own family's information.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
