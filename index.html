Your holiday companion

Vayanza

Find what’s worth doing on your trip, save what you like, and keep your holiday relaxed rather than timetabled.

Coming soon to iPhone

What it does

  • Suggestions for your trip. Places to eat, things to do and places worth seeing, chosen for your holiday and checked against Google so they’re real places with real travel times from where you’re staying.
  • Save now, decide later. Keep ideas without a date, or loosely place them on a day. No rigid itinerary.
  • Routes from your hotel. Put your saved places in a sensible order and open directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps.
  • Memories that stay yours. Your holiday photos stay in Apple Photos on your iPhone. Stories are made on your phone.

Privacy in brief

  • Your photos and videos are never uploaded to Vayanza.
  • Your location is used only when you choose to use it. Vayanza doesn’t track you in the background.
  • You can delete your account and its server data from within the app.

A full privacy policy will be published here before the app launches.

How Vayanza may earn money

For some activities, Vayanza may show a link to check availability and book with a partner such as Viator. If you book through that link, Vayanza may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Bookings are made with the partner, not with Vayanza.

Partners can’t pay to be recommended. Commissions never affect which places Vayanza suggests or the order they appear in.
